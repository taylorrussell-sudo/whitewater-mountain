## Remaining limitations, privacy and backups

A live road still covers only its field of view. Ski-road conditions beyond the turnoff and official road reports remain outside this integration. DriveBC metadata and the image watermark can briefly differ at a frame change. The app labels the feed update, uses the provider stale flags and caps display age. Seasonal continuity at individual cameras remains unverified. No Whitewater imagery is collected by the app. No live weather records, source images or review captures are committed.

The latest DriveBC still is held in RAM and excluded from backups. SQLite retains source check state and the daily budget under an additive schema migration. A restart may wait until the next eligible camera check. Existing weather retention and synthetic backup verification apply. Production backup coverage and restore on the Pi remain separate work.
