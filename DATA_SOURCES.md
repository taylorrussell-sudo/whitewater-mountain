## Whitewater snowfall, snowpack and mountain observations

- **Verified on Conditions.** Public HTML includes overnight, 24-hour, 48-hour, three-day, seven-day and total snowfall in cm, plus current base depth in cm. The report identifies the **1,950 m Summit weather plot**. Keep overnight and 24-hour fields independent, preserve the source's “total snowfall” label and confirm the season reset and each window cutoff. Current base is depth, with its own field. The same page shows summit and base temperatures (°C), wind speeds (km/hr) and compass directions. The snow report's update label is adjacent to the report. The summary weather readings have no separately visible valid times. [Conditions](https://whitewatermountainresort.com/conditions/)
- **Verified on Advanced Weather.** Public HTML shows separate latest-24-hour tables for **Summit Top (2,002 m)** and **Whitewater Base (1,630 m)**. Both show hourly date/time labels, air temperature (°C), wind speed (km/hr), prevailing direction (degrees and compass), and RH (%). Summit also shows pressure with no unit in the header. Leave pressure out until documented. The resort flags raw sensor readings as potentially missing or erroneous. A reviewed snapshot showed the newest rows one hour apart between stations, so collect and assess each station separately. The two pages' wind readings cannot be paired by value or assumed to share averaging periods. [Advanced Weather](https://whitewatermountainresort.com/advanced-weather/)
- **Timestamp and siting gaps.** The Conditions report shows a date, year and clock time, while Advanced Weather rows show month/day and time without a year. Neither display gives an explicit offset. The public report review found updates on successive mornings, but the measurement cutoff, guaranteed cadence, off-season availability, wind sampling and sensor coordinates remain unconfirmed. Confirm clock conventions with the resort before inferring a year or converting a local timestamp. B.C. moved to year-round UTC−07:00 Pacific time in 2026, and a data provider may still use a different convention. The fixture uses explicit offsets. Store source dates with their meaning and retain UTC retrieval time independently. [B.C. time change](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026AG0013-000209)
- **Permission decision.** Section 2 of the resort terms requires express written permission for copying and redistribution of downloaded material. The public pages establish human access. No documented public numerical API, reuse licence or automated cadence was found. Robots policy retrieval was inconclusive and would not establish reuse rights. Ask the resort for a supported route, written permission covering only individual measurements, cadence, attribution, private display and historical retention including backups, plus station identifiers and timestamps. The PoC models the fields using synthetic data and has no Whitewater HTTP adapter or HTML parser. Link to the official report while measurements are unavailable. The separately credited RWDI forecast and cameras require separate validation. [Resort terms](https://whitewatermountainresort.com/terms-of-use/) · [Resort contact](https://whitewatermountainresort.com/contact-us/)

The documented Avalanche Canada Weather API returned station **22**, external ID **STHRDG**, with matching coordinates and elevation, source **BC Ministry of Transportation & Infrastructure** and a display timezone of `America/Vancouver`. The newest was about one hour old. All rows supplied air temperature (°C), average wind (km/h), direction (degrees and compass) and RH (%). Snow depth (cm) was present in 22 rows and `newSnow` in eight. The API describes `newSnow` in cm, but the station's measurement window, method and missing-value semantics remain open. Use the station as a nearby exposed ridge temperature and wind candidate. Preserve the ridge identity and exposure for comparisons with Nelson. Whitewater lodge weather and resort snowfall still require their own sources. [Weather API documentation](https://avalanche.ca/api-docs)
- Avalanche Canada's Data Sharing Policy invites public API exploration. The documented API and policy give no explicit grant for recurring automated caching, private display or retention of this third-party station's data. Leave collection off until Avalanche Canada or the provincial owner confirms those uses, request frequency, attribution and quality flags. ## Open-Meteo hourly forecasts

- **Permitted for this intended use.** The free API terms cover personal home automation and private non-commercial apps under CC BY 4.0, with limits below 600 calls/minute, 5,000/hour and 10,000/day. Link Open-Meteo beside displayed data, link the licence, identify adaptations such as computed snowfall windows, and carry model credit. Start with two point requests every three hours (16/day) for an initial single-model trial in the future service. Treat 403 as a stop, honour `Retry-After` for 429, and back off on transient failures. There is no paid dependency. [Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) · [Licence](https://open-meteo.com/en/licence)
- **Documented request.** GET `https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast`, with `models=gem_global`, `timezone=GMT`, `timeformat=unixtime`, `forecast_hours=169` and explicit metric temperature/wind. Target points are lodge **49.443, −117.146, 1,630 m** (approximate base-area point inferred from location references, with sensor coordinates unverified), and Nelson CS **49.491389, −117.305278, 534.9 m**. Request `temperature_2m` (°C), `snowfall` (cm), `wind_speed_10m` (km/h) and `freezing_level_height` (m above sea level). Preserve the returned grid centre separately from the request coordinates and the returned adjustment elevation. GEM Global remains the default for reproducing the original probes. The diagnostic also accepts explicit model selections. [API contract](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) · [GEM model details](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/gem-api) · [Base-area map](https://whitewatermountainresort.com/site/assets/files/5309/wh20_master_plan_addendum_2019_final_1.pdf)
- **Times and math.** Documented JSON has parallel time/value arrays and an hourly units object. Snowfall at time `t` is the modeled accumulation for the preceding hour ending at `t`. Accumulate the first complete UTC bins after the next whole-hour boundary, which may omit the remaining fraction of the current hour. The PoC computes 24/48/72-hour totals and the maximum of all 49 complete 24-hour windows starting 0–48 hours from that boundary. A gap or null makes affected totals unavailable. Missing candidates suppress the maximum and ties select the earliest start. The Forecast API's generation duration in milliseconds is request processing time. Exact model issue time is unknown from this contract. Retrieval age detects stale cache, while model-run freshness requires a verified run-level source. [Hourly definitions](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs#hourly_parameter_definition) · [Model update notes](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/model-updates)
- **Live lodge and valley probes.** Both GEM Global responses returned JSON and 169 matching consecutive Unix-second slots in UTC, with seven-day coverage of temperature, wind and snowfall. Both had all 24/48/72-hour snowfall totals and the complete 72-hour rolling search. The model returned lodge grid centre **49.500, −117.150** at the requested adjustment elevation of **1,630 m** and valley centre **49.500, −117.300** at **534.9 m**. Every tested model returned all-null freezing-level values with unit `undefined`. The adapter accepts this sentinel only for the entirely null freezing-level field. Leave freezing level unavailable pending a validated source or profile calculation. [Probe commands](poc/README.md)

### Forecast model comparison

Each model was requested individually at both points. Every response had 169 matching UTC slots. “Populated” counts apply to temperature, snowfall and wind at each point. Snow windows require every bin, so the short-range models had only the 24-hour total in this sample. The temperature comparison is the mean absolute difference from IFS 9 km on 24 matched future hours at each site. These are inter-model differences from a single snapshot. [Open-Meteo GEM documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/gem-api) · [Open-Meteo ECMWF documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/ecmwf-api)

| Model (request ID) | Grid / nominal horizon / updates | Populated hours | Complete snow totals | Temperature difference vs IFS 9 km (lodge / valley) |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| GEM Global / GDPS (`gem_global`) | ~15 km / 10 days / 12 h | 169 | 24/48/72 h | 0.7 / 1.6 °C |
| GEM Regional / RDPS (`gem_regional`) | 10 km / 84 h / 6 h | 81 | 24/48/72 h | 1.5 / 1.4 °C |
| GEM HRDPS Continental (`gem_hrdps_continental`) | 2.5 km / 48 h / 6 h | 45 | 24 h | 0.9 / 0.6 °C |
| GEM HRDPS West (`gem_hrdps_west`) | Experimental 1 km / 48 h / 12 h | 39 | 24 h | 0.6 / 1.9 °C |
| ECMWF IFS HRES (`ecmwf_ifs`) | 9 km / up to 15 days / 6 h | 169 | 24/48/72 h | Reference |
| ECMWF IFS open-data grid (`ecmwf_ifs025`) | 0.25° (~25 km) / up to 15 days / 6 h | 169 | 24/48/72 h | 0.4 / 0.3 °C |
| ECMWF AIFS Single (`ecmwf_aifs025_single`) | 0.25° (~28 km) / up to 15 days / 6 h | 169 | 24/48/72 h | 0.6 / 0.9 °C |

- **Extended outlook.** Trial IFS HRES 9 km for the seven-day temperature, wind and snow outlook, with GEM Global or RDPS as a separately labelled comparator. RDPS covered 72 hours here. Its remaining horizon depends on run age. Open-Meteo interpolates output to a URL-requested hourly grid. GDPS is natively three-hourly, IFS 9 km is hourly through forecast hour 90 before coarser steps, and AIFS Single is six-hourly. IFS 0.25° and AIFS are optional comparison guidance. Both returned the same grid centre for lodge and valley with distinct adjustment elevations. [GEM documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/gem-api) · [ECMWF documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/ecmwf-api)
- **Local outlook.** Evaluate operational HRDPS 2.5 km first for the next day. The experimental 1 km West model covers both points and can provide additional terrain guidance. Its slower run cadence and experimental status limit its suitability as a sole source. Keep forecast comparisons within matching valid times and preserve each model's identity. Both short-range forecasts had null tails. Avoid filling those tails with zero or silently extending a series with another model. Direct ECCC GRIB2 can provide run provenance and additional variables if warranted, with separate processing and usage-policy work. [ECCC HRDPS](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/msc-data/nwp_hrdps/readme_hrdps_en/) · [Experimental West data](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/msc-data/nwp_hrdps/readme_hrdps-datamart-alpha_en/) · [Open-Meteo Single Runs API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api)
- **Terrain and verification.** A named grid remains an area forecast, even with elevation adjustment. Compare same-model lodge and valley samples at matching valid times. Local inversion, wind and precipitation phase can be poorly resolved. Compare over several events with eligible site-specific observations before calibrating snowfall, snow alerts or inversion confidence. None of the tested forecasts supplies measured resort snowfall.

The probe filters `msc_id-value=1145M29` over a six-hour UTC interval, sorts by `-date_tm-value` and limits to six records. Check WMO ID and geometry against reference metadata; longitude precedes latitude and height can be present. Accept finite `air_temp` in °C with QA 100. For a systematic local archive, evaluate MSC Datamart AMQPS and persist only permitted station history. MSC policy reserves Datamart HTTP downloads for ad hoc retrieval. ## Simulated inversion inputs

Every scenario includes a mock Nelson valley temperature paired with the existing simulated lodge value at scenario start. The inversion scenario also has paired mock forecast temperatures at +0, +3, +6, +9, and +12 hours. The other scenarios currently provide upper-mountain outlooks without a comparable valley forecast. The inversion comparison keeps observations and forecast samples separate and treats missing, invalid, or time-mismatched pairs as unavailable.

The source elevations are provisional. A future implementation needs station and model provenance, comparable valid times, freshness checks, and validated elevations. The layer artwork is illustrative. Neither the sample values nor the forecast 0°C level establishes the shape or depth of a cold-air layer.

The inversion scenario has one mock sky comparison (observed at scenario start: Nelson fog, lodge clear). The skiing context uses this only if its type and valid hour match the temperature pair. No forecast sky samples are supplied. Future sky/visibility, wind, precipitation and snow-surface inputs require separate validated sources and timestamps; an inversion alone cannot establish sunshine or snow quality.

## Private-use validation policy

Prioritize individual numerical observations from Whitewater (snowfall, snowpack, temperature and wind), official public API/feed discovery, source terms and permitted request frequency. Private use reduces redistribution concerns but is not blanket permission to automate collection. Do not copy proprietary narratives, bypass restrictions or collect/display prohibited material. Where applicable terms are materially unclear, document the blocker and leave automated retrieval disabled until clarified. Evaluate RWDI, cameras and other content separately. Keep live records and raw payloads out of GitHub. Validate model and observation timestamps, attribution, retention and permitted storage; a forecast or model-derived snow amount is never a measured resort report. Request only the minimum needed data, cache conservatively, and stop on access or rate-limit errors.
