Understanding the dashboard

Transparent logic, source attribution and the roadmap. Prototype v0.1.1 · 26 November 2026.

01 / Elevation & freezing level

The 0°C level is a forecast atmospheric elevation. It is not the existing snowpack boundary or a reliable rain/snow boundary. Inversions can break a simple vertical temperature relationship. The profile is schematic, not a trail map. Elevations are planning values; verify Glory's lower terminal and Nordic trailhead before live release.

02 / Powder alert logic

Blue solid ring = reported snowfall; violet dashed ring = forecast snowfall. Alerts use 24-hour totals, not multi-day accumulation. Forecast alert uses the largest modeled rolling 24-hour total in the next 72 hours; the time window is shown. The glow begins to pulse slowly at 15 cm. Reduced motion keeps the glow static. These thresholds are initial defaults, not official resort definitions.

03 / Data & prediction methodology

Observed = measured or officially reported. Forecast = provider model. Calculated = our interpretation. Unavailable = missing, invalid or stale. Everything numerical in v0.1.1 is simulated. Future models will be kept separate, including differences in elevation and precipitation type. Forecast agreement is not a calibrated probability.

04 / Roadmap & feedback

  • Prototype: responsive layout, five tabs, adjustable scenarios, themes, animated alerts, interactive explanations.
  • Next: family design feedback; validate data availability and permissions.
  • Live winter: actual snow, elevation forecasts, webcams, avalanche bulletins, grooming, road conditions, timestamps and stale-state behavior.
  • Powder intelligence: adjustable thresholds, overnight/opening-time totals, forecast comparison, season history, inversion and rain-on-snow flags.
  • Wall release: isolated local app, lightweight cached service, touchscreen tests, explicit backup and rollback coverage.
  • Summer later: AQHI, PM2.5, smoke, heat, rainfall, alpine weather and remaining snowpack. Mountain bike data optional.
