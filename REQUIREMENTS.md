# Mountain Dashboard requirements

Status — v0.3.4 proposes deployment reliability and runbook updates for the private Pi installation. The public v0.2.1 prototype remains simulated.

## Core winter experience
- Whitewater-centric overview with five internal tabs: Overview, Powder, Webcams, Avalanche, Info. Main wall application eventually includes Mountain alongside Calendar, Home, Music and Lists.
- Schematic elevation profile from Nelson through Apex/Nordic, Glory base, Day Lodge, individual chair tops, snow plot and Ymir Peak. Elevations provisional until verified; distinguish freezing level from falling snow transition and actual snow cover. Show changing freezing level and elevation-specific forecasts.
- Show temperature by elevation for comparable Nelson valley and Whitewater Lodge pairs. Keep measured, simulated and forecast indicators separate, show their signed lodge-minus-valley difference and valid times, and label missing or mismatched pairs unavailable. Interpret ≤0°C as no positive inversion signal, >0°C to <3°C as weak, 3°C to <6°C as notable, and ≥6°C as strong. These are provisional dashboard thresholds.
- Highlight a positive observed signal with a schematic cold-air layer of unspecified depth. Where consecutive comparable forecast samples bracket a transition, show illustrative onset or dissipation. Show a duration range only when both transitions bracket the same run. Keep inversion independent of freezing level, powder, and avalanche indicators.
- For positive inversion signals, add compact, conditional skiing context (relative cold, near/above-freezing lodge temperature and potential snow softening). Show a valley-fog / clear-lodge contrast only with comparable sky observations or forecasts of the same time and type. Missing evidence stays unavailable. No prediction of snow quality, sunshine, visibility, road safety or avalanche danger from temperature differences alone. Keep the existing forecast timing brackets and explain dissipation.
- Show reported overnight/24h/48h/3-day/7-day/season snowfall and snowpack where available; lodge/summit temperature and wind; seven-day forecasts and cumulative predicted snow next 24/48/72h. Preserve observation site and time.
- Separate actual and predicted powder alerts, icy-blue solid vs violet dashed visual language. Use a static accent at 5 cm, a soft glow at 10 cm, and an increasingly visible slow pulse from 15 cm. Keep the glow static when reduced motion is enabled. Configurable thresholds remain planned. Proposed 24h tiers: below 5 cm normal, 5–<10 fresh, 10–<15 watch, 15–<25 powder, 25–<40 major, 40+ exceptional. Forecast uses maximum rolling 24h snowfall within the next 72h, not a multi-day total. Display expected timing and confidence when supported.
- Powder quality is qualitative and experimental; use temperature, wind, snowfall timing, freezing level and previous weather only when grounded. Never imply avalanche safety.
- Selectable webcam gallery with visible keyboard focus retained on selection and camera controls at least 44×44 CSS px. Add source attribution and timestamps when approved. Show three elevation bands with numeric and text ratings plus distinct severity accents. Add the official region and bulletin expiry when validated and retain the official link; stale/missing = unavailable, not low. Nordic grooming and access road conditions when validated.
- Info explains thresholds, data provenance, forecast vs observed vs calculated, uncertainty, limitations, and interactions. Update explanations with logic changes.
- Responsive phone layouts down to 320 CSS px and a 1920×1080 wall layout; no scrolling for essential desktop overview, touch-sized controls, clean muted design, light/dark/auto, accessible motion.
- One application/codebase and one private household live deployment. The existing public simulated Pages site is temporary feedback only; retire it after verifying private hosting and before any public live-data access. GitHub/Codex fixture-based development is not a second deployment. Local service collects authorized data, caches with provenance/freshness, and stores permitted history; family access by LAN or separately approved private authenticated network. No public Pi exposure or paid services. Validate source terms, privacy, resource impact, rollback and backup coverage before production; exact implementation and access changes require separate approval.

- Keep the primary IFS 9 km outlook and HRDPS 2.5 km guidance separate. Use ECCC Nelson CS for measured valley temperature. Provide freezing-level guidance from an independently attributed vertical profile, preserving multiple supported crossings and leaving unbracketed heights unavailable. Keep Southridge collection disabled pending an eligibility review. The private API must include source provenance, valid and retrieval times, freshness and missing-data status.

Allow explicit opt-in for the two publicly accessible Whitewater pages below, limited to individual factual measurements and their provenance. Whitewater’s published terms restrict uses of site content. Applicability to recurring private extraction of facts is unresolved. Record the terms and access evidence in [Data Sources](DATA_SOURCES.md#whitewater-snowfall-snowpack-and-mountain-observations).
- Check the snow report every two hours and the mountain observations every hour, including when requested through Refresh Data. Apply provider spacing, rate limits and backoff. Retain only the latest normalized snapshot per source, with a bounded private cache. Preserve source update and observation times independently of retrieval time. Keep raw pages and proprietary material outside the cache, and live records out of GitHub.
- Assess Whitewater imagery separately. Open the official webcam gallery from the private Webcams tab in a new tab and keep the five individual stills available through official links. Revisit in-app display when the resort documents permission and a supported method. If direct browser display becomes eligible, use only the selected official image, with normal browser caching and a 15-minute interval or a slower cadence if required by the provider. Keep Whitewater imagery out of service requests and storage. [Webcam review](DATA_SOURCES.md#whitewater-webcam-integration-review-before-v033)
- Use synthetic values and original placeholders for publicly reviewable screenshots. Keep private screenshots and residence or network details out of GitHub.

## Private live dashboard and refresh

- Use ECMWF IFS HRES for the seven-day lodge forecast and rolling 24-hour powder alert. Show accumulation for 24, 48 and 72 complete forecast hours. Label Canadian HRDPS independently as short-range guidance and preserve missing hours.
- Display QA-accepted Nelson CS measured temperature with its station valid time. Show modeled lodge values as forecasts. Keep missing measurements unavailable. Use fresh Whitewater Base readings for measured lodge temperature. Pair with the closest fresh Nelson CS measurement within 30 minutes and show both valid times.
- Display every resolved NOAA GFS 0 °C crossing and exact-zero altitude band, including separate representations for a sampled profile with no crossing and insufficient coverage. Derive forecast inversions from matching, same-model lodge and valley hours. Keep missing hours out of the inversion timing brackets.
- Show source credit, licence, freshness, last checked, last successful check and available source valid times. Keep stale values out of active indicators and retain last successful cache entries on failed refreshes.
- Send Refresh Data through the same private scheduler as automatic collection. Allow one manual batch at a time with a server-wide 60-second request cooldown and enforce provider request spacing, retry due times and stop periods. Show progress and per-source outcomes. Require a same-origin, validated POST on the loopback service.

- Show the licensed DriveBC junction still in Overview and Webcams, with source update time, stale and unavailable states, official attribution and an accessible enlarged view. Preserve camera selection across refreshes, tab switches and reloads. Keep unapproved feeds link-only and preserve the static simulation.
- Persist a six-batch daily UTC limit on early weather checks. Charge the batch before its first eligible early weather request. Keep due checks and eligible camera checks independent of this budget. Display the balance and skipped outcomes.

## Raspberry Pi installation

- Use immutable reviewed releases, a dedicated service account and a separate persistent state directory on verified NVMe. Run the Python service under systemd with bounded resources and boot startup. Measure Chromium and service impact on the Pi 5 with Calendar, Home Assistant and Music Assistant running.
- Keep the API on IPv4 loopback. Private ingress requires a verified household LAN binding and subnet and optionally authenticated, restricted Tailscale Serve. Require an exact approved Host and Origin for refresh and verify isolation from guest and public clients.
- Default every source to disabled. Enable sources individually after approval and keep per-provider cadence, backoff and manual-refresh limits.
- Reserve the LAN address in DHCP, verify HTTP readiness with bounded retries, and check automatic startup and ingress after reboot. If using the optional Mountain firewall, load its table before starting the LAN edge and preserve unrelated host rules.
- Keep a minimal encrypted backup of installed app configuration, release identification and systemd units. Exclude application code and runtime data. For approved SQLite recovery drills, use checked snapshots and restore to a fresh path. Verify release rollback and actual recovery on the Pi after approval. Keep the standalone simulation and existing Pages preview until private operation is verified and retirement is approved. [Deployment runbook](deploy/pi/README.md)
